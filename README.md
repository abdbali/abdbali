## About Me

I see technology as a learning medium rather than an end in itself; sharing knowledge, producing open-source educational materials, and mentoring young developers define my core mission.

My work centers directly on **instructional design**, **coding for kids and youth**, **STEM projects**, **community-driven open curriculums**, and **AI communication**.

---

## Education & Mentorship Focus

- **Algorithmic Thinking & Core Programming:** Hands-on workshops developing problem-solving skills through Python and block-based platforms.
- **STEM & Physical Computing:** Interdisciplinary projects integrating Arduino, sensor interfacing, and scientific discovery.
- **Applied Data & AI Literacy:** Foundational data analysis, practical machine learning, and generative AI guides for the next generation of builders.
- **Open-Source Curriculums:** Community-focused educational resources and guides built for self-paced learning.
